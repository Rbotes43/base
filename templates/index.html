About This Tool

This tool helps you review advertising content against the UK ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) Code. It identifies potential compliance issues and provides suggestions for improvement.

  • High Risk: Issues that likely breach ASA Code and require immediate attention
  • Medium Risk: Claims requiring substantiation or clarification
  • Low Risk: Minor issues that should be addressed for best practice
